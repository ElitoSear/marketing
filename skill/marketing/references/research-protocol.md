# Research protocol

Trends move in weeks. Limit research to roughly the last 90 days. Record findings with dates in the feature's inspiration directory (`marketing config`, for carousels `carousel.inspirationDirectory`) as `inspiration-YYYY-MM.md`; create or extend. No directory configured: keep notes in the conversation.

## Source order

1. **Direct observation** of real posts and ads. Strongest evidence.
2. **Primary sources** with a date: platform announcements, executives' own posts.
3. **Secondary blogs** only as hypotheses, flagged as such. Many are SEO pages.

State sample size and bias: one feed skewed to design content is not "Instagram".

## Step 1: web GET tools

Start with fetch, curl or any web-GET tool. Reach what is public:

- Dated trend writing and platform announcements.
- Public pages of competitors and adjacent tools.
- Meta Ad Library (`https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/`): often JavaScript-rendered, may return little.
- Brand's own public profile (handle from `marketing config`).

Report what came back empty or blocked. Do not pretend a blocked page was read.

## Step 2: browser, with permission

Text cannot show a slide. If a browser tool is available (Chrome extension, Playwright, similar), ask the user before using it:

> May I research in the browser myself? Read-only: I open competitor posts and ads, page through slides, and look at them. I never log in, like, follow or save.

Proceed only after a yes. On a no or no browser, go to step 3.

What to study with a browser: the brand's account (Saved, Home, Explore, Reels; the owner's taste), competitor and adjacent carousels page by page, Ad Library results by keyword, active ads, sorted by impressions. Note advertiser, start date, format, hook.

## Step 3: user screenshots

Ask the user to drop screenshots in a folder. Analyse with image understanding: palette, type, layout, density, imagery, swipe device.

## Browser rules (non-negotiable)

- Read only. No like, follow, save, comment, message, subscribe or ad clicks. Opening a post and paging through slides is fine.
- Never ask for or enter a password. Use the session the user already has open. On a login wall or challenge, stop and tell the user.
- Human pace, small volume: a dozen posts, not hundreds. No crawling, loops or scraping services. Close tabs you opened.
- Copy the idea, never the artwork or script. Credit what inspired a decision in the notes.

## Per observation, write down

Account or advertiser, date seen, what stops the scroll, how the swipe is carried, type and imagery, CTA, why it works. Add one line on what you will do differently.

## Go beyond this file

If something suggests a device, format or hook not covered here, chase it. This file is a floor, not a ceiling.

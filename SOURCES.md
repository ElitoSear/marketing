# Sources

The skills in `skill/` are original text. Some of their guidance was distilled from public agent skills and marketing material, with the ideas rewritten and nothing copied. This file records where, for maintainers; it is not shipped in the package.

Community adoption (GitHub stars) was the signal used to choose sources, and none of them publishes campaign performance. Statistics some of them quote, such as retention thresholds, are unverified and were left out. Platform numbers (safe zones, limits) drift with app updates and should be re-checked now and then.

## Carousel structure, hooks and ranking signals (read 2026-10-06)

MIT-licensed skill packs: `Jakeschincariol/instagram-agent-skill` (carousels, viral research, scored hook formulas), `sergebulaev/instagram-skills` (slide spines, algorithm heuristics, humanizer) and `itchernetski/threads-carousel-claude-skill` (slide types and style axes). Their ranking claims are community-reported, not official.

## Narrative structures, hook moves and platform text (read 2026-10-09)

The `social` and `ad-creative` skills of a marketing-skills pack installed in the author's projects (hook system, static and video format taxonomy, platform text limits, vertical-video safe zones, caption and audio defaults, motion-video quality checks) and its `video` skill (reverse-engineering an edit into a beat sheet), and the `design-taste-frontend` skill (anti-template design rules).
